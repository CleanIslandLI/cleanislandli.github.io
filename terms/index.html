Booking terms

Estimates and final prices

Online prices are estimates based on the sizes you enter. We confirm the final price with you on site before work starts. If the confirmed price is higher than your estimate, you may decline and your deposit will be refunded in full.

Deposits

A $50 deposit is required to confirm a booking. It is applied to your final bill. Requests that need our review are not charged until we approve them and you pay the deposit.

Payment

Payments are processed by Stripe. The remaining balance is due when the work is finished, through a secure payment link. We never see or store your full card number.

Cancellations and rescheduling

You can cancel or reschedule up to 48 hours before your appointment for a full deposit refund or transfer. With less than 48 hours' notice, the deposit is non-refundable. If we cancel or reschedule for weather or any other reason, your deposit is refunded or moved to the new date, as you prefer.

Weather

We may reschedule for storms, high winds, or temperatures too low to wash safely. We'll contact you as early as possible.

Your property

Please provide access to the areas booked and a working outdoor water spigot, close windows and doors, and move vehicles and fragile items. We'll point out existing damage such as loose paint, failing caulk or rotted wood before starting. Washing can reveal or loosen these existing conditions.

Results

Some stains, such as deep oil, rust, oxidation and sun fading, may lighten but not disappear. Service pages list each service's limits.

Off-season

We operate April 1 through October 31. Bookings outside these dates are available only when we open them.
