Questions and answers

Everything about pricing, scheduling, prep and payment.

Pricing

How do you price a job?

By size: square feet for houses and flat surfaces, linear feet for fences and gutters, and per item for things like windows and bins. You see the same rates we use, and the booking form adds it up for you. Every job has a $150 minimum.

Is the online price final?

It's an estimate based on the sizes you enter. We confirm the final price on site before any work starts. If it would come out higher, you can say no and your deposit is refunded.

Do you charge for travel?

No travel fee for towns within about 15 miles of Islip. Towns about 15 to 25 miles away have a $25 travel fee. Farther than that, send a quote request and we'll let you know.

Do you offer discounts for more than one service?

Yes. Booking two main services in the same visit takes 7% off them, and three or more takes 12% off.

Scheduling

How do I book?

Choose your services, enter sizes, pick a date and time window, and pay the $50 deposit. Most smaller jobs are confirmed right away. Larger or unusual jobs come to us as a request, and we confirm within one business day.

When are you open?

We wash from April 1 through October 31, Monday to Saturday. In the off-season you can still reserve a spring date online.

Do I need to be home?

No, as long as we can reach the areas you booked and an outdoor water spigot. Tell us about gates, dogs and codes in the access notes.

Weather

What happens if it rains?

Light rain doesn't affect most washing. For storms, high wind or temperatures that are too low, we reschedule at no charge and your deposit carries over.

Preparation

How should I prepare?

Close all windows and doors, move cars out of the driveway, bring in pets, and move fragile items, toys and decorations away from the areas being cleaned. We handle the rest.

Water access

Do you need my water?

Yes. We connect to an outdoor spigot. A typical house wash uses about as much water as a long shower or a load of laundry or two. Let us know if your spigot doesn't work.

Cleaning methods

Do you use high pressure on siding?

No. Siding, painted surfaces, stucco and wood are cleaned at low pressure with a detergent that does the work. Higher pressure is used only on hard surfaces like concrete.

Surface safety

Is the cleaner safe for my plants and pets?

We wet plants before we start and rinse them again after, which dilutes any overspray. Keep pets inside until surfaces dry.

What won't you clean?

Roofs, the inside of gutters, asphalt driveways, and anything that needs a tall ladder or roof access. We also don't stain, seal or repair surfaces.

Payment

How do I pay?

A $50 deposit by card holds your date and is taken off your bill. After the job, we send a secure Stripe link for the balance. Card details go straight to Stripe and are never stored by us.

Cancellations

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Cancel or reschedule 48 hours or more before your appointment and your deposit is refunded or moved. With less than 48 hours' notice the deposit isn't refundable, unless we cancel for weather.

Seasonal availability

Can I book in winter?

Winter dates are closed because water freezes on surfaces. You can reserve any open date in the coming season now, and early bookings get the best choice of dates.

Still have a question?

Send us a message or a quote request and we'll get back to you.
