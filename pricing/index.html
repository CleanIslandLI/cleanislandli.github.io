How estimates become final prices

Your online total is an estimate based on the sizes you enter. When we arrive we check the sizes and the condition and confirm the price with you before we start. If it would be higher than your estimate, you can decline and your deposit is refunded.

Very large homes, heavy staining, unusual surfaces or properties outside our usual area go through a quote request instead.

Travel

No travel fee for towns within about 15 miles of Islip. A $25 fee applies to towns about 15 to 25 miles away. See which towns are in each zone.
