# Clean Island brand

**Positioning:** the easy, honest choice for Long Island homeowners. Published prices, real online booking, and the right pressure on every surface. The website, the truck, the yard signs and the invoices should all say the same thing: *clean, careful, clear prices.*

## Logo
- **Mark:** a water drop in Harbor Navy. Inside it, a white wave crest over blue water reads as both "washing" and the shoreline, and the white highlight suggests a fresh, clean surface. It works at favicon size and on a shirt.
- **Wordmark:** "Clean Island" in heavy, wide Archivo (confident, like lettering on a work truck), over "POWER WASHING" in spaced, blue Public Sans.
- **Files:** `assets/img/logo.svg` (on light), `logo-white.svg` (on navy), `logo-mark.svg` (icon, social avatar, magnets).
- Give the logo clear space at least the height of the drop on all sides. Don't stretch it, recolor it, or put it on busy photos.

## Color (brand psychology)
| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Harbor Navy | #0E2A47 | Trust and competence. Text, headers, truck lettering |
| Deep Tide | #081B30 | Footer, dark backgrounds |
| Bay Blue | #1F62D0 | Action. Buttons and links only, so "Book" always stands out |
| Spray | #9CC7FA | Accents on navy |
| Foam | #E6F0FC | Light highlights |
| White | #FFFFFF | Cleanliness. The dominant color |

Blue reads as clean water and dependability, and navy reads as an established, careful company. Use one action color everywhere so customers learn what to tap.

## Type
- Headlines: **Archivo** (Google Fonts, weight 800 to 850, width about 115%).
- Text: **Public Sans** (Google Fonts, 400 to 700). It's clear, neutral and easy to read on phones.

## Signature visual
The **clean stripe**: one bright band washed across grimy concrete, the same stripe a pressure washer leaves on a driveway. Use it on yard signs, door hangers, social posts and the truck: a dirty texture with one clean diagonal band and the headline inside it.

## Voice
Plain, specific and honest. Say what's included and what isn't. Never invent reviews, years in business or guarantees. Use real before-and-after photos as soon as you have them.

## Marketing mix (4 Ps), briefly
- **Product:** the full exterior clean-up for a home, done in one visit. Bundles make "do the whole property" the easy choice.
- **Price:** published and slightly below established competitors to win the first season, with a $150 minimum and bundle discounts that protect margins. Raise prices after you have reviews.
- **Place:** Islip-centered zones with no travel fee nearby keep drive time low and jobs per day high. Online booking runs all year, even in winter.
- **Promotion:** Google Business Profile with reviews, door hangers on streets where you've just finished a job ("we just cleaned your neighbor's house"), yard signs at completed jobs, Nextdoor and local Facebook groups, and early-spring promo codes created in /admin.
