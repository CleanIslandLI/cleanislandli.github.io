# Starter equipment and launch checklist

This is the cheapest setup that can do every service on the website. Prices are typical 2026 retail and vary by store and sale. The list covers one crew; double the starred items when you run two crews at once.

## Must-have gear (about $1,050 to $1,250)

| Item | Why | Approx. cost |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Gas pressure washer, about 3,200 PSI and 2.5 GPM, with triplex or axial pump (e.g. Powerwasher PW61430-S, Westinghouse WPX3200, BE BE327PX) | Does everything on the site. 2.5 GPM is the minimum for a surface cleaner to work well | $330 to $400 |
| ★ 15" surface cleaner rated for 2.5 GPM, 3,000+ PSI, quick-connect | Fast, streak-free driveways and patios, which makes flatwork profitable | $100 to $150 |
| ★ 50 ft, 3/8" high-pressure replacement hose and quick connects | Stock 25 ft hoses are too short to reach around a house | $60 to $80 |
| ★ Downstream chemical injector and black soap tip | Applies house-wash solution at low pressure (house and fence washing) | $25 to $35 |
| ★ Telescoping extension wand, 18 to 24 ft | Reaches second-story siding and gutter faces from the ground | $80 to $120 |
| ★ 2 × 50 ft, 5/8" garden hoses (supply) | Connects the customer's spigot to the machine | $50 to $70 |
| ★ 2-gallon chemical-resistant pump sprayer | Spot treatments, oil pre-treat, wood cleaner | $20 to $30 |
| Soft brush on an extension pole, deck brush, bin brush | Hand-cleaning doors, trim, furniture, bins | $50 to $70 |
| Window kit: scrubber, 14" squeegee, short pole, towels | Exterior windows | $50 to $70 |
| 6 ft fiberglass stepladder (skip if you have one) | Windows and safe low reach. Never use it on roofs | $90 to $120 |
| PPE: chemical gloves, safety glasses, rubber boots | Required with bleach-based cleaners | $40 to $60 |
| Plant covers or tarps, 5-gallon buckets, gas can, fuel stabilizer, pump saver | Protects landscaping and the pump | $50 to $70 |

## First chemical order (about $120, enough for 15 to 25 jobs)

- Sodium hypochlorite 10 to 12.5% (pool or liquid chlorine from a pool supply store, about $6 to $10 a gallon). Diluted through the downstream injector, it's the active ingredient in house wash.
- House-wash surfactant or soap made for downstream use, $25 to $40.
- Concrete degreaser for oil spots, $20 to $30.
- Oxygen-based wood cleaner (sodium percarbonate) for decks and wood fences, $25 to $35.

**Safety:** never mix bleach with acids, ammonia or other cleaners. Rinse plants before and after. Read every label. Keep chemicals off pool water and away from pets.

## Before taking paid jobs (not legal advice: confirm each item)

1. **Liability insurance.** Get quotes for general liability; $1M per occurrence is common for this work. This protects you if a window cracks or water gets inside. Once you're insured, tell Claude and the site will say so.
2. **Workers' comp and disability.** New York requires both once you have employees. Your two employees mean this applies.
3. **Licensing.** Nassau County lists power washing as work that requires a home improvement license. Suffolk's rules are less clear, so call the Suffolk County Department of Labor, Licensing & Consumer Affairs to ask whether residential power washing needs a home improvement license. The website currently lists Suffolk towns only.
4. **Register the business.** A DBA (assumed name certificate) is the minimum for using "Clean Island Power Washing." An LLC adds liability protection. Then get a free EIN at irs.gov and open a business bank account for Stripe payouts.
5. **Sales tax.** In New York some cleaning services on real property can be taxable, so ask an accountant before your first season. If you need to collect it, Stripe and the price list can be updated.

## Pricing notes (why the numbers are what they are)

- Local guides put Long Island pressure washing at roughly $80 to $2,100 depending on the job. A Massapequa company reports $200 to $600 for a standard home.
- Our house wash, about $300 for a 2,000 sq ft home, sits in the lower middle of that range. It wins jobs as a new company while covering labor, chemicals, fuel and equipment wear.
- The $150 visit minimum covers setup, travel and teardown. Single small driveways pay the minimum, and the bundle discounts steer customers toward adding services.
- After about 10 jobs, compare your actual hours per job against the prices. Raise them in /admin if any service takes longer than expected.
