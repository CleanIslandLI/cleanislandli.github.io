Homes in our area

On the South Shore, from Babylon to Patchogue, salt air and bayside humidity grow mildew fast on north-facing siding and shaded patios. Many homes from West Islip to Sayville benefit from a yearly house wash in spring. Farther north, in Smithtown, Commack and Hauppauge, heavy tree cover means more pollen, sap and algae on decks, fences and walkways.
