Patios, walkways and pool decks

Patios, walkways, pool decks, front porches and steps cleaned of algae, moss, mildew and dirt so they look good and grip better.

Shaded patios and walkways grow slick algae that's both ugly and slippery. We clean flat surfaces with a surface cleaner where it fits, and by wand on steps, edges and tight spaces.

Around pools, we keep cleaner out of the water and rinse the deck thoroughly when we finish.

What's included

  • Moving lightweight furniture and planters, then putting them back
  • Cleaning the full surface, edges and steps
  • Spot attention to moss in joints and corners
  • Front entry, porch floor, stoop and steps when you add them
  • A final rinse of the area and the surfaces next to it

Surfaces we clean

  • Concrete patios and walks
  • Pavers
  • Bluestone and flagstone
  • Pool coping and concrete pool decks
  • Brick steps and stoops

What to expect

Green and black growth, dirt and mildew come off, and the surface looks clean and grips better under foot.

Limits

  • We don't seal or re-sand stone or pavers.
  • Soft or crumbling mortar joints can wash out. We keep pressure low and avoid them.
  • We don't drain, clean or treat pools.
