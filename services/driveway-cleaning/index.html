Driveway cleaning

Concrete and paver driveways cleaned evenly with a rotary surface cleaner, with edges and joints finished by hand-held wand.

A clean driveway changes how the whole house looks from the street. We clean the main field with a rotary surface cleaner, which leaves an even finish without the zebra stripes you get from a wand alone.

Edges, aprons, joints and the strip along the garage are finished by hand-held wand, then everything is rinsed toward the street.

What's included

  • Sweeping off loose debris and moving small items out of the way
  • Surface cleaning of the full driveway field
  • Edging along lawns, beds, the garage and the curb apron
  • Pre-treatment of oil or rust spots when you add it
  • Final rinse of the driveway and nearby walks

Surfaces we clean

  • Poured concrete
  • Concrete and stone pavers
  • Exposed aggregate (adjusted pressure)
  • Brick pavers

What to expect

Dirt, mildew, algae, tire grime and organic stains lift, and the concrete brightens noticeably. Older oil and rust stains usually fade, but they may not fully disappear.

Limits

  • We don't re-sand or seal pavers. Washing can loosen some joint sand, which we point out.
  • We don't clean asphalt or blacktop driveways.
  • Deep oil, rust, tire marks and fertilizer stains can be lightened, not guaranteed removed.
  • Cracked or crumbling concrete can lose loose surface material.
