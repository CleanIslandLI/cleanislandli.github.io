Windows, gutters and garage doors

Hand-cleaned exterior windows, gutter faces and fascia washed from the ground, and garage doors cleaned to match a fresh house wash.

These are the details that finish a house wash. Windows are cleaned by hand with a scrubber and squeegee where they can be reached safely from the ground or a short ladder.

Gutter faces and fascia are washed from the ground with an extension wand and brush, which removes the dirt and mildew streaks on the outside of the gutter.

What's included

  • Exterior glass scrubbed, squeegeed and detailed, with sills and frames wiped
  • Gutter faces and fascia washed and rinsed
  • Garage door panels, trim and windows washed at low pressure

Surfaces we clean

  • Standard exterior glass
  • Aluminum and vinyl gutters
  • Painted or wrapped fascia
  • Steel, aluminum and composite garage doors

What to expect

Clearer glass, gutters without black streaks, and doors and trim that match the rest of a clean house.

Limits

  • We don't clean the inside of gutters or downspouts.
  • We don't clean interior windows, repair screens, or work on windows that need a tall ladder.
  • Black "tiger stripe" streaks on old gutters may only lighten.

Pricing

  • Gutter and fascia exterior wash$1 per linear ft, $100 minimum
  • Garage door$35 per door
