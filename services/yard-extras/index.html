Sheds, play sets, furniture and bins

The rest of the yard: sheds, play sets, patio furniture, mailboxes and trash bins, cleaned while we're already there.

Most of these are quick add-ons that make the whole property look finished. Trash bins are cleaned inside and out and rinsed, which cuts down on smell and bugs in summer.

What's included

  • Shed walls, doors and trim washed (shed roofs excluded)
  • Play sets and swing sets washed at low pressure and rinsed well
  • Furniture frames and hard surfaces cleaned (cushions excluded)
  • Trash and recycling bins washed inside and out
  • Mailboxes and posts cleaned

Surfaces we clean

  • Vinyl, wood and metal sheds
  • Wood and plastic play sets
  • Resin, metal and wood furniture
  • Plastic bins

What to expect

Mildew, bird droppings, dirt and grime come off, and bins come back clean and rinsed.

Limits

  • Bins, mailboxes and stain pre-treatment are add-ons and need at least one main service.
  • We don't clean fabric cushions, umbrellas or canvas.
  • Old weathered plastic can stay faded after cleaning.
