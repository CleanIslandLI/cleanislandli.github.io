Deck and fence cleaning

Wood and composite decks and vinyl or wood fences cleaned with low pressure and a wood-safe cleaner, never blasted.

Wood is soft. Too much pressure raises fibers, leaves gouges and makes boards look worse once they dry. We use a wide fan tip at low pressure and let a wood-safe cleaner do the work.

Vinyl fences get the same low-pressure detergent wash we use on siding, which takes off the green film without scratching.

What's included

  • Clearing the deck and protecting nearby plants
  • Cleaner applied to boards, railings, stairs and posts
  • Low-pressure rinse with the grain of the wood
  • Fences washed one side or both sides, as you choose

Surfaces we clean

  • Pressure-treated pine
  • Cedar
  • Composite decking (follow manufacturer limits)
  • Vinyl fences
  • Wood privacy and picket fences

What to expect

Mildew, algae and grime come off, and wood looks fresher and more even. Graying from sun is lightened, not fully reversed.

Limits

  • We don't stain, seal, sand or repair decks or fences.
  • Old peeling stain may loosen and look patchy after washing.
  • Rotted or soft boards are pointed out and skipped.
