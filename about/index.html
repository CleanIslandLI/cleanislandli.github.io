About Clean Island

A new, locally owned power-washing company based in Islip.

Clean Island Power Washing is run by two partners and a small crew from Islip. We started it to give Long Island homeowners a simple way to get their homes cleaned: clear prices you can see before you book, a real calendar, and careful work.

We're a new business, and we'd rather tell you that than invent a history. What we can promise is how we work.

How we work

  • The right pressure for each surface. Detergent and low pressure on siding, wood and paint, and higher pressure only on concrete.
  • Prices published online and confirmed with you on site, before any work begins.
  • Plants and landscaping wet down before we start and rinsed after.
  • Before and after photos of every job.
  • We point out loose paint, damaged boards or anything else we notice before we start.

What we don't do

We don't clean roofs, work at heights that need tall ladders, or offer restoration work like sealing and staining. If a job isn't something we can do well, we'll say so.
