Common questions

How do you price a job?

By size: square feet for houses and flat surfaces, linear feet for fences and gutters, and per item for things like windows and bins. You see the same rates we use, and the booking form adds it up for you. Every job has a $150 minimum.

Is the online price final?

It's an estimate based on the sizes you enter. We confirm the final price on site before any work starts. If it would come out higher, you can say no and your deposit is refunded.

When are you open?

We wash from April 1 through October 31, Monday to Saturday. In the off-season you can still reserve a spring date online.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain doesn't affect most washing. For storms, high wind or temperatures that are too low, we reschedule at no charge and your deposit carries over.

Do you use high pressure on siding?

No. Siding, painted surfaces, stucco and wood are cleaned at low pressure with a detergent that does the work. Higher pressure is used only on hard surfaces like concrete.

How do I pay?

A $50 deposit by card holds your date and is taken off your bill. After the job, we send a secure Stripe link for the balance. Card details go straight to Stripe and are never stored by us.
